# Platform brand assets

The source cards use official platform artwork strictly to identify where each saved source originated.

- `InstagramBrandIcon`: full-color gradient glyph from Meta's [Instagram brand asset pack](https://www.meta.com/brand/resources/instagram/instagram-brand/).
- `YouTubeBrandIcon`: full-color red digital icon from YouTube's [brand resources](https://brand.youtube/youtube-icon).

The PNGs were downloaded from the official packs on September 6, 2026 and resized proportionally for the iOS asset catalog. Their colors, artwork, and clear space were not otherwise modified. Each icon appears inside the source card that links directly to the corresponding platform content.
